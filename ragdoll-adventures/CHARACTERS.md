# Character Bible

Status: unnamed siblings approved by owner. Use sister_01 and brother_01 internally; neither calls the other Brother/Sister in spoken dialogue.

## Shared identity
Brother and sister; living handmade crochet rag dolls, never human children. Blue embroidered eyes, pale yarn skin, long soft limbs, brown yarn hair and visible stitches. Their sibling bond is kind, playful and cooperative. Stories show discovery and problem-solving through action and conversation.

## Approved animated environment

Cinematic 3D animated storybook gardens, homes and paths with rounded sculpted scenery, colourful foliage, soft light and coherent shadows/scale. Match the softer animated crochet/material finish in The Leaf Boat - Animated Garden Test while preserving the original dolls' identity. Do not exaggerate rugged fibres, distressed yarn or photorealistic surface detail. The later Continuity Revision is rejected; see [STYLE_LOCK.md](STYLE_LOCK.md). This animated format replaces the earlier photographic real-world surroundings requirement.

Lock one specific environment and its geography for connected scenes. A river stays the same river; no unshown change to a pond, puddle or unrelated stream. Preserve landmarks, flow/travel direction, weather and lighting. See [CONTINUITY.md](CONTINUITY.md) for mandatory motion and join checks.

## Continuity rules
Keep the photographs as identity references. A banner is branding, not a replacement character reference. Verify actual media entries and record their IDs before generation. Never identify them only by newest item or filename guess.
Both characters may share grey shoe colours; preserve the actual dolls rather than imposing upstream disjoint wardrobe colours.
Keep proportions, embroidery, clothing and yarn colours consistent. Do not add human skin, realistic teeth, extra dolls, limbs or wardrobe swaps.
