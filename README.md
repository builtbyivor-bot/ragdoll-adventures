# Ragdoll Adventures

![Ragdoll Adventures](ragdoll-adventures/assets/banner.png)

Two handmade rag-doll siblings discover small wonders, solve everyday problems and share warm, funny adventures through **their own dialogue**.

**Status:** workflow adapted; names and voices awaiting selection; no episode or voice audition generated yet.

International, family-friendly storytelling, accessible roughly to ages 4–10. This is creative positioning; publishing-platform audience designations are decided separately. English should be clear and conversational, without forced slow-English lesson delivery. Exact accent will be auditioned.

## Start here

1. Choose the siblings' names and personalities in [Character Bible](ragdoll-adventures/CHARACTERS.md).
2. Audition and approve two distinct voices using [Voice Setup](ragdoll-adventures/VOICES.md).
3. Make a short dialogue/lip-sync proof, then plan the first episode.
4. Run [the adapted VideoExpress workflow](SYSTEM_PROMPT.md).

The sister's pink dress, brown pigtails and purple ties and the brother's turquoise stripes and upright brown yarn hair remain their identities. Preserve crochet stitches and handmade proportions.
